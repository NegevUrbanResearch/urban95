Beer Sheva Accessibility Map

  • Three Analysis Modes

    Use the Building, Neighborhood, and City toggle to switch between scales. Building mode lets you inspect individual buildings, Neighborhood mode compares areas across the city, and City mode shows a full overview with rankings and distributions.

  • Building Mode

    Click anywhere on the map to select the nearest building. A walking-distance isochrone will appear showing the reachable area, with a count of nearby amenities. Toggle between raw counts and percentile ranking to see how a building compares to the rest of the city.

  • Neighborhood & City Modes

    In Neighborhood mode, click any neighborhood polygon to see its detailed breakdown — amenity mix, per-type averages, and citywide percentile rankings. City mode opens a dashboard with amenity distribution, score histograms, and a full neighborhood ranking.

  • Filter & Walking Time

    Use the filter dropdown to focus on specific amenity types such as healthcare, education, or recreation. Switch between 5, 10, and 15 minute walking times to see how accessibility changes at different distances.

This map is a collaboration between Urban95 and NUR as part of the "Cities for Children" initiative, exploring how accessible Beer Sheva's neighborhoods are for families with young children.

Methodology

Each building's accessibility score is calculated by counting amenities reachable within walking-time isochrones (5, 10, or 15 minutes) computed from the pedestrian street network. Trees are included but weighted at 1/4 the value of other amenities. Building colors use a percentile scale — each color band represents an equal share of buildings, so relative differences are visible regardless of the count range. Neighborhood and city-level statistics are aggregated from these per-building scores.

Data

The map uses building footprints, points of interest including healthcare facilities, educational institutions, commercial areas, and recreational spaces, street tree locations, and neighborhood boundaries throughout the city.
